My journey is about staying curious, getting the fundamentals right, and turning ideas into practical digital experiences. I like the process as much as the result: asking questions, working through a tricky bug, and learning something new along the way.

I also share bits of that journey online — because a lesson learned is even more useful when someone else can learn from it too.
